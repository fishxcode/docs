The Codex Pro Pool Monthly Pack runs on the official Codex Pro account pool. You buy a fixed amount of credit for one month at a time, which suits high-frequency Codex coding and agent development. It is currently the only plan series on sale and comes in three tiers:

| Plan | Price | Plan credit | Effective cost multiplier | Validity | Credit reset | Best for |
| - | - | - | - | - | - | - |
| $2,000 Credit Monthly Pack | $260 | $2,000 | 0.13x | 1 calendar month | No reset | High-frequency development and enterprise teams |
| $200 Credit Monthly Pack | $30 | $200 | 0.15x | 1 calendar month | No reset | Mid-range credit, the top pick for everyday development |
| $20 Credit Monthly Pack | $3.60 | $20 | 0.18x | 1 calendar month | No reset | Entry-level trial with flexible use |

Prices and availability are subject to the [pricing page](https://fishxcode.com/pricing).

## How to Use It After Purchase

1. Buy the Codex monthly pack with the amount of credit you need.
2. Select the `codex-sub-june` group in the console.
3. Create a new API token in that group.
4. Configure the token in a supported client, such as [Codex](/en/codex) or [Codex Desktop](/en/codex-desktop), and you're ready to go.

## Billing Rules

- The `codex-sub-june` group has a fixed **1x multiplier**.
- Effective cost multiplier = plan price ÷ plan credit.
- Requests deduct plan credit based on actual token usage.
- The plan is valid for 1 calendar month.
- Plan credit does not reset, and unused credit does not carry over automatically.

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
  If you pay $260 for $2,000 in credit, the effective cost multiplier is **0.13x**, but the group still shows a 1x multiplier.
</Callout>

## Notes

<Callout type="warn" title="Only the codex-sub-june group can use monthly pack credit">
  For the Codex monthly pack, you must create tokens in the `codex-sub-june` group. Other groups cannot use the pack's credit.
</Callout>

- The monthly pack is not unlimited. Once the credit runs out or the plan expires, you can no longer use it.
- If you'd rather not buy a monthly pack, you can also call the Codex series groups pay-as-you-go from your balance. See [Billing Rules and Groups](/en/billing-groups).
